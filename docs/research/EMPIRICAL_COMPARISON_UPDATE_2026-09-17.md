# World3 / Limits to Growth Empirical-Comparison Update

Date: 2026-09-17
Status: research input; not model qualification

## Scope and provenance

This update was produced while the Consensus research connector was unavailable. The literature pass used SciSpace semantic search and direct publisher pages as independent retrieval routes. Tool availability does not change the evidentiary status of any paper or claim.

The purpose is not to decide whether World3 is "right." It is to extract methodological obligations for World Zero's comparison spine.

## 1. Herrington 2021

Source:
- Gaya Herrington, *Update to limits to growth: Comparing the World3 model with empirical data*, Journal of Industrial Ecology 25(3), 614-626.
- DOI: `10.1111/jiec.13084`.

Source-derived finding:
- empirical series were compared against four Limits to Growth scenarios using updated data;
- two scenarios were reported as the closest alignments in that update;
- the scenario that had aligned closest in earlier comparisons was not among the two closest in the updated comparison;
- the paper notes that scenario trajectories diverge substantially more after 2020.

World Zero interpretation:
- trajectory alignment is evidence about comparative fit, not proof that the model's causal mechanisms are uniquely identified;
- the changing closest scenario is a warning against retrospective narrative lock-in;
- increasing post-2020 scenario separation creates a stronger future discrimination surface, provided observations and comparison rules are frozen independently of outcomes.

Evidence status for World Zero use: `INFERRED` methodological implication grounded in a scholarly empirical comparison.

## 2. Pasqualino et al. 2015

Source:
- Roberto Pasqualino, Aled W. Jones, Irene Monasterolo, Alexander Phillips, *Understanding Global Systems Today—A Calibration of the World3-03 Model between 1995 and 2012*, Sustainability 7(8), 9864-9889.
- DOI: `10.3390/su7089864`.

Source-derived finding:
- the authors calibrated World3-03 against historical data for 1995-2012;
- they explicitly state that some key World3 quantities, including physical-limit variables such as non-renewable resources and persistent pollution, are not easily assessed from available data;
- because accurate data on physical limits were unavailable, the paper did not claim to assess overshoot to those global limits directly.

World Zero interpretation:
- an observation surface can be inadequate even when other model variables calibrate well;
- lack of observability must remain an explicit identifiability/observation-model limitation rather than being silently converted into parameter certainty;
- structural conclusions about poorly observed stocks require distinct evidence from fit on better-observed growth variables.

Evidence status for World Zero use: `INFERRED` methodological implication grounded in a scholarly calibration study.

## 3. Crescenzi et al. 2024 — Earth for All sensitivity analysis

Source:
- Pierluigi Crescenzi, Giorgio Gambosi, Lucia Nasti, Aurora Rossi, Emanuele Natale, *A sensitivity analysis of the Earth for all model: Getting the giant leap scenario with fewer policies*, Journal of Industrial Ecology 28(6), 1481-1492.
- DOI: `10.1111/jiec.13582`.

Source-derived finding:
- the study applies both global Sobol sensitivity analysis and local sensitivity analysis to the Earth for All model;
- it reports that a substantially smaller policy subset can approximately reproduce the six target-indicator trajectories of the larger "Giant Leap" policy package under the study's scenario design.

World Zero interpretation:
- model complexity must earn its keep;
- local and global sensitivity analysis should be treated as complementary, because parameter interactions can be missed by one-at-a-time perturbation alone;
- if a simpler mechanism/policy subset reproduces the claimed behavior, the larger mechanism set should not receive automatic explanatory credit;
- this directly supports World Zero's mechanism-admission, ablation and benchmark-comparison gates.

Evidence status for World Zero use: `INFERRED` methodological implication grounded in a peer-reviewed sensitivity-analysis study.

## Resulting obligations for the scientific comparison spine

1. Never translate visual or numerical historical alignment directly into causal validation.
2. Preserve exact observation provenance and upstream lineage so correlated derived datasets cannot inflate apparent confirmation.
3. Freeze holdouts, metrics and tolerances before confirmatory execution.
4. Carry `OBSERVATION_MODEL_INADEQUATE` and other identifiability states explicitly when key stocks or mechanisms are not directly constrained by data.
5. Require both local and global sensitivity analysis where interactions can materially alter conclusions.
6. Preserve simpler non-causal and reduced-form benchmarks; expensive causal machinery must demonstrate claim-specific value beyond them.
7. Require ablation evidence before promoting a mechanism into F7 minimal synthesis.
8. Treat post-2020 divergence among historical World3 scenarios as an opportunity for discrimination, not as permission to retrofit scenario definitions after seeing new observations.

## Non-claim

This update does **not** establish that World3 predicted current conditions, that collapse is occurring, that a particular World3 scenario is true, or that World Zero has predictive validity. Those require separately frozen subjects, admitted data cuts, comparison protocols, and out-of-sample evidence.
